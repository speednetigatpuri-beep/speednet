How to recharge online

  1. Open the Customer Login page.
  2. Log in with your Customer ID and registered mobile number.
  3. Choose your plan and validity. You'll see the exact amount, including GST, before paying.
  4. Pay by UPI, debit/credit card, net banking or wallet through our payment gateway.
  5. You get a payment status and receipt on screen, and the recharge is activated automatically. See our Service Delivery Policy.

Don't know your Customer ID? Call or WhatsApp +91 94222 14143 from your registered mobile number and we'll share it.

Plans available

  • Home broadband — fiber plans at different speeds, with unlimited data.
  • Business broadband — for shops, offices, hotels and resorts.
  • IPTV — live TV channel packages over your internet connection.

Plans are available with monthly and yearly validity. Installation is free on yearly recharge. The current price of each plan, with GST, is shown in the customer portal after login and is also shared on call or WhatsApp.
