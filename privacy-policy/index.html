2. Information we collect

When you visit this website

  • You can use this website without creating an account or filling in any form.
  • The "Check if we cover your area" PIN code check works inside your own browser. The PIN code you type is not sent to us or stored by us.
  • Our hosting and security provider (Cloudflare) automatically processes basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you open, so that the website can be delivered quickly and protected from attacks.

When you contact us

  • If you call us or message us on WhatsApp, we receive your phone number, your name (if shown or shared) and the details you send us, such as your address or the type of connection you need.

When you take an internet connection

  • Name, installation address, phone number and email address.
  • Identity and address proof and a photograph, as required by Government of India rules (customer verification / KYC) for internet connections.
  • Billing, recharge and payment records.
  • Details of the equipment installed (such as router and fiber device) and the service you use.
  • Technical and usage records of your connection (such as IP address allotment and session logs) that internet service providers are required to keep under their licence conditions.

When you pay online

  • Online payments are processed by our payment gateway partner. We receive only the payment details needed for your account, such as the transaction ID, amount, date and payment status.
  • We do not receive or store your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking password.

3. How we use your information

  • To check availability, install, provide and maintain your internet connection.
  • To give customer support and fix faults.
  • To send bills, recharge reminders and important service messages by call, SMS or WhatsApp.
  • To keep our network safe and prevent misuse or fraud.
  • To meet our legal obligations, including telecom licence conditions and lawful requests from government authorities.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone for their own advertising.

4. Who we share it with

  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as our billing and customer-management software provider, payment gateway / payment providers and technicians working on our behalf. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Network partners who supply our internet bandwidth, only where needed to deliver your service.
  • Government and law-enforcement authorities, when required by law, a licence condition or a valid legal order.

5. Third-party services on this website

This website uses a few services from other companies. When you use them, those companies may collect information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps (map of our office) and Google Fonts (text styles).
  • Google reviews links, which open our Google Business Profile.
  • WhatsApp chat buttons, which open WhatsApp (Meta).
  • Cloudflare, which hosts and protects this website.

6. Cookies

This website does not use its own advertising or tracking cookies. The embedded Google Map and other third-party services listed above may set their own cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

7. How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as you are our customer and afterwards only for as long as we need it for billing, resolving disputes, or meeting legal and telecom licence requirements. After that, we delete it or make it anonymous.

8. How we protect information

We use reasonable security measures to protect personal information, including access controls on our systems and limiting access to staff who need it for their work. No system is completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

9. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • Ask for a summary of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your information, where we are not required by law to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent you have given us (this may affect our ability to provide the service).
  • Raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer (below), and if not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children

Our internet connections are provided to adults (18 years or older). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.
