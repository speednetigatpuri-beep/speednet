Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SpeedNet Internet Solutions ("SpeedNet", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you visit speednetonline.in, contact us, pay online or use our website design, development and automation services. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian laws.

2. Information we collect

When you visit this website

You can use this website without creating an account. Our hosting and security provider (Cloudflare) processes basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you open, to deliver the website and protect it from attacks. The map on our home page is provided by Google Maps and the fonts by Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when they load.

When you use the contact form

The project brief form does not send anything to our servers. It opens WhatsApp on your device with your message ready, and you decide whether to send it. If you send it, we receive your WhatsApp number and the details you typed.

When you contact us or become a client

Name, business name, phone number, email address, and the project details, content and account access you share with us.

When you pay online

Online payments are processed by our payment gateway partner. We receive only the details needed for our records, such as your name, contact details, quote / invoice number, transaction ID, amount, date and payment status. We do not receive or store your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking password.

Data inside your project

When we build a CRM or automation, we may handle your customers' data (for example names and phone numbers) on your behalf. We use it only to build, test and support your project, as you instruct, and we do not use it for anything else.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to you, prepare quotes and deliver your project.
  • To process payments, send receipts and keep accounts and tax records.
  • To give support and send important messages about your project by call, email or WhatsApp.
  • To keep this website secure and to meet legal requirements.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

Only with service providers we need to deliver our work, such as hosting (Cloudflare), the payment gateway, WhatsApp and email providers, and with government authorities when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep project and payment records for as long as needed for the project, support and accounting and tax laws. Data inside your project is returned or deleted when you ask, or when the project ends, unless the law requires us to keep it.

6. Security

We use secure (HTTPS) connections, access controls and trusted providers to protect information. No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to keep your data safe.

7. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. Contact us using the details above.

8. Grievance Officer

See our Contact & Grievance page. We respond within 30 days.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page with the date it was updated.
