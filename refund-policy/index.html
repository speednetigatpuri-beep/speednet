Refund policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains when payments to SpeedNet Internet Solutions for website design, development, automation and CRM services can be refunded.

1. Failed payments

If money is deducted but the payment shows as failed, the amount is usually reversed automatically by your bank or the payment gateway within 5–7 working days. If it is not reversed in that time, contact us with your transaction ID. After we verify it, we either mark the payment as received or refund it within 7 working days.

2. Duplicate or extra payments

If you are charged twice, or pay more than the invoice amount, the extra amount is refunded to your original payment method within 7 working days after verification. If you prefer, we can adjust it against your next invoice.

3. Cancelling before work starts

If you cancel a project in writing before we start work on it, the amount you paid for that project is refunded in full within 7 working days.

4. Cancelling after work has started

If you cancel after work has started, we charge for the work completed up to the date of cancellation, as per the stages in your quote. Any amount paid for work not yet started is refunded within 7 working days.

5. When refunds are not possible

  • Work that has been completed and delivered or approved.
  • Amounts paid to other companies on your behalf, such as domain names, hosting, WhatsApp Business API fees or software licences, once purchased.

6. How to ask for a refund

Call, WhatsApp or email us within 15 days of the payment with your name, mobile number, quote / invoice number, payment date, amount and transaction ID.
