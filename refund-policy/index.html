Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what happens if an online payment to SpeedNet Internet Solutions fails, is made twice, or is made for the wrong amount.

1. Failed payments (money deducted, recharge not done)

  • If money is deducted but the payment shows as failed or the recharge is not activated, the amount is usually reversed automatically by your bank / payment gateway within 5–7 working days.
  • If it is not reversed in that time, contact us with your transaction ID. After we verify it, we either activate your recharge or refund the amount within 7 working days.

2. Duplicate payments

  • If you are charged twice for the same recharge, the extra amount is refunded to your original payment method within 7 working days after verification.
  • If you prefer, we can adjust it against your next recharge instead.

3. Wrong amount or wrong plan

  • If you paid more than the plan price, the extra amount is refunded or adjusted against your next recharge.
  • If you chose the wrong plan and it has not been activated, contact us within 24 hours and we will change the plan or refund the amount.

4. When refunds are not possible

  • Once a recharge has been activated and the service is in use, the amount is not refundable for the used or remaining validity, except where required by law.
  • Installation charges (where applicable) are not refundable once installation is done.

5. How to ask for a refund

Call, WhatsApp or email us within 15 days of the payment with your Customer ID, registered mobile number, payment date, amount and transaction ID.
