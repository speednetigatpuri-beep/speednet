How pricing works

  1. Tell us what you need on call, WhatsApp or the contact form.
  2. Get a written quote with the work included, the total price in Indian Rupees (₹), GST where it applies, the payment schedule and the timeline.
  3. Pay by UPI, bank transfer or a secure payment link we send you, as per the payment schedule in your quote.
  4. Get your receipt on email or WhatsApp after the payment is confirmed.

Running costs

Some services have running costs paid to other companies, such as domain names, hosting, or WhatsApp Business API message fees. These are listed separately in your quote so you know the full cost before you start.

Why no fixed prices? Every business needs something different. A quote means you pay only for what you need. Payments follow our Terms, Refund Policy and Cancellation Policy.
