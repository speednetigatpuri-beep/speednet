Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

SpeedNet Internet Solutions provides digital services (internet and IPTV). We do not ship physical products. This policy explains when your service starts after you pay.

1. Recharge of an existing connection

  • After a successful online payment, your recharge is activated automatically, usually within a few minutes.
  • In rare cases (for example, a delay in payment confirmation from the bank), activation can take up to 24 hours.
  • You get the payment status and receipt on screen after payment. If the recharge is not active within 24 hours, contact us with your transaction ID.

2. New connection

  • After you contact us, we check that our network is available at your address.
  • Once customer verification (KYC) and payment are complete, our engineer installs the connection, usually within 2–3 working days, depending on location and cabling work needed.
  • Installation is done at your address by our own technicians. Installation is free on yearly recharge.

3. Service area

We provide services in Igatpuri, Ghoti and nearby areas of Nashik district, Maharashtra, where our network is available.

4. Delays or problems
