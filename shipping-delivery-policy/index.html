Shipping & delivery policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

SpeedNet Internet Solutions provides digital services (website design and development, WhatsApp and LinkedIn automation, social media management, AI automation and CRM). We do not ship physical products. This policy explains how and when your service is delivered.

1. Payment confirmation

After a successful online payment you see the payment status on screen. We send a receipt on email or WhatsApp, usually the same day and always within 24 hours. If you do not get it, contact us with your transaction ID.

2. How we deliver

  • Websites: shared first on a preview link for your review, then published on your domain after your approval.
  • Automations and CRM: set up on your accounts or hosting, tested with you, and handed over with logins for your team.
  • Social media management: posts are shared with you for approval and published on your pages as per the agreed calendar.
  • Files and access: login details and any files are shared securely by email or WhatsApp.

3. Delivery time

Work starts after the first payment in your payment schedule is received. The delivery timeline is written in your quote and depends on the size of the project and on getting your content, access and approvals on time.

4. Where we deliver

Our services are delivered online, so we can work with businesses anywhere in India. Meetings can be on call, WhatsApp or at our office in Igatpuri.
