Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains when a recharge or connection with SpeedNet Internet Solutions can be cancelled.

1. Cancelling a recharge

  • A recharge can be cancelled with a full refund only if it has not been activated yet. Contact us within 24 hours of payment.
  • Once a recharge is activated, it cannot be cancelled and the validity continues until it ends.

2. Stopping your connection

  • You can stop your internet or IPTV service at any time by calling or WhatsApping us.
  • Your service continues until the end of the current recharge validity. There is no refund for unused days of an activated recharge.
  • Equipment provided by us (such as the fiber device or router) must be returned when the service is closed.

3. Cancellation by SpeedNet

We may cancel or suspend a connection for misuse, illegal activity, unpaid dues, failed customer verification, or if required by law or a government authority. If we cancel a recharge before activation for reasons on our side, we refund the full amount.
