Cancellation policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how a project or service with SpeedNet Internet Solutions can be cancelled.

1. Cancelling a project

You can cancel a project at any time by telling us in writing on WhatsApp or email. Refunds for cancelled projects follow our Refund Policy: a full refund if work has not started, and payment only for completed work if it has.

When a project is cancelled, we hand over the work completed and paid for up to that date.

2. Ongoing services

For ongoing services such as hosting management, maintenance or automation support billed monthly or yearly, you can cancel at any time. The service continues until the end of the period already paid for. There is no refund for the unused part of a period that has started.

3. Cancellation by SpeedNet

We may cancel or pause a project or service if payments are overdue, if content or use is illegal or harmful, if information or access needed for the work is not provided for a long time, or if required by law. If we cancel for reasons on our side, we refund any amount paid for work not delivered.
