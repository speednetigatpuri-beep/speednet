Questions

How much does a website or automation cost?

It depends on what you need. Tell us about your business and we'll send a clear written quote before any work starts. See how pricing works.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the project. You get a timeline with the quote, and you can follow progress on a preview link while we build.

Can you manage our social media posts?

Yes. We plan a monthly content calendar with you, design the posts, schedule them on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn, and send a short monthly report.

Is LinkedIn automation safe for my profile?

We only use methods that follow LinkedIn's rules, such as scheduled posts and saving leads to your sheet or CRM with reminders. No fake accounts or bulk messaging bots.

Can WhatsApp messages be sent automatically?

Yes. We explain the options (WhatsApp Business API or a WhatsApp Web sender) and their limits before we set anything up.

Do you help with domain and hosting?

Yes. We help you buy and set up the domain and hosting, and connect Google Search Console and your Google Business Profile.
