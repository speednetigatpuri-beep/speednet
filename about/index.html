About SpeedNet

A local business in Igatpuri that builds websites and automations for other businesses.

Who we are

SpeedNet Internet Solutions is a proprietorship based in Igatpuri, Nashik district, Maharashtra. We also run a fiber internet service in Igatpuri and Ghoti.

Today we build websites and set up automation and social media for other businesses, such as Brawnepak and GOCHCK.

What we do

  • Website design: the look and layout of your website, made for phones first.
  • Website development: fast, secure websites with SEO basics, set up on your domain and hosting.
  • WhatsApp automation: reminders, receipts, order updates and staff alerts.
  • LinkedIn automation: profile set-up, scheduled posts, and leads saved with follow-up reminders.
  • Social media management: content calendar, post designs, scheduling and monthly reports.
  • AI automation: chatbots, custom CRMs and workflows between forms, sheets, email and billing.

How we work

Every project starts with a conversation and a written quote that lists the work, the price and the timeline. You see progress on a preview link, and we launch only after you approve. See Pricing for how quotes and payments work.
